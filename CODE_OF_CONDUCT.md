# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Short version: be kind, assume good faith, critique work rather than people, and help newcomers. Engineers at every level are welcome here, from people learning their first FFmpeg flag to people who wrote the standards.

Report concerns to the maintainers privately ⟨add contact address⟩. Reports are handled confidentially.
